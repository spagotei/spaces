# Safety & Enforcement

Spaces uses reports, automated signals, human review, account security systems, and community moderation to keep the platform safe.

## Enforcement levels
1. Education or warning
2. Content or feature restriction
3. Temporary account or Space restriction
4. Permanent account or Space termination
5. Emergency action where immediate risk requires it

Actions are not required to follow every level. Severe or urgent violations may result in immediate action.

## Appeals
Where an appeal process is available, use the in-app Support system. Appeals should include the relevant account or Space, the action being appealed, and any information that may materially change the decision. Repeated abusive or fraudulent appeals may themselves be restricted.

## Staff safety
Spaces staff will never ask for your password, recovery code, one-time authentication code, or private encryption key. Verify official support links and sender domains before providing sensitive information.
