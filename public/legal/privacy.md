# Spaces Privacy Policy

**Effective date: October 7, 2026**

This Privacy Policy explains what information Spaces may collect, why we use it, how we protect it, when we disclose it, and the choices available to you. It is intended to be read with the Spaces Terms of Service.

> **Important:** This is a product-ready draft and should be reviewed against Spaces' actual data flows, vendors, retention periods, regional privacy laws, and legal entity before publication.

## Information we collect
Depending on the features you use, Spaces may process account information, authentication information, profile information, Space membership and role information, messages and files you choose to send, device and client information, security logs, support tickets, reports, and technical diagnostics.

## How we use information
We use information to provide and maintain the Services, authenticate accounts, deliver messages and notifications, operate communities, provide Support, prevent abuse and fraud, secure the platform, investigate reports, troubleshoot problems, improve features, comply with law, and communicate with you about your account or the Services.

## Content and private communications
Content is processed to deliver the features you request and to maintain safety and reliability. Access to private or support content should be limited to authorized systems and personnel with a legitimate operational or safety need, subject to applicable law and Spaces policy.

## Sharing
Spaces may use vendors and service providers to host infrastructure, deliver email, provide analytics or diagnostics, process payments, secure accounts, and operate other platform functions. Spaces may disclose information when required by law, to protect people or the Services, to enforce policies, or in connection with a business transfer, subject to applicable law.

## Retention
Retention periods depend on the type of information, its purpose, legal requirements, security needs, and user controls. Spaces should publish specific retention periods once production data maps and deletion jobs are finalized.

## Your choices
Depending on your region, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, export, restrict, or object to certain processing. You may also control notifications and certain privacy settings in Spaces. Requests can be submitted through the Legal Center or Support.

## Security
Spaces uses technical and organizational safeguards designed to protect information. No service can guarantee absolute security. Report suspected vulnerabilities through the official security channel rather than attempting to access another user's information.

## Children
Spaces does not knowingly permit use in violation of applicable minimum-age requirements. If you believe a child has provided personal information in violation of applicable rules, contact Spaces through the Legal Center.

## Changes
We may update this Policy as the Services and laws change. The current version and effective date will be posted in the Legal Center.
