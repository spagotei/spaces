# Spaces Partner Code of Conduct

Partners represent the quality of the Spaces community. Partner owners, administrators, and moderators are expected to act responsibly on Spaces and when publicly representing their Partner relationship.

## Community standards
Maintain active moderation, respond to credible safety reports, treat members respectfully, and do not encourage harassment, raids, hate, threats, or other prohibited conduct.

## Authenticity
Do not buy, sell, transfer, or misrepresent Partner status. Do not materially repurpose a Partner Space in a way that makes its application or public description misleading without notifying Spaces.

## Original work
Do not represent copied or infringing work as your own. Respect copyright, trademarks, privacy, and publicity rights.

## Official representation
Use Partner branding only as provided or approved by Spaces. Do not imply that Spaces sponsors a product, statement, event, or transaction unless Spaces has expressly approved that representation.

## Enforcement
Spaces may issue warnings and remediation deadlines, pause benefits, or remove Partner status. Serious violations may result in immediate action.
