# Spaces Community Guidelines

These Guidelines apply across Spaces, including public and private communities, direct messages, profiles, files, usernames, and other platform features.

## Treat people with respect
Do not harass, threaten, stalk, target, or organize abuse against other people. Do not coordinate raids or ban evasion intended to harm a person or community.

## No exploitation or sexual abuse
Never sexualize, exploit, groom, or facilitate abuse of minors. Child sexual abuse material is strictly prohibited and may be reported to appropriate authorities where required or permitted by law.

## No credible threats or violent extremism
Do not make credible threats of violence, coordinate real-world harm, or support violent extremist activity. Fictional or game content is treated according to context and applicable safety rules.

## No scams or malicious activity
Do not phish, steal accounts, distribute malware, impersonate trusted services to steal information, or use Spaces to facilitate fraud.

## Respect intellectual property
Only share material you have the right to share. Do not use Spaces to distribute infringing or counterfeit material.

## Keep communities safe
Space owners and moderators are expected to maintain reasonable moderation, use permissions responsibly, and respond to reports. Partner and Official Spaces have additional expectations.

## Enforcement
Spaces may warn, remove content, restrict features, suspend accounts, restrict Spaces, or permanently terminate access depending on severity and history.
