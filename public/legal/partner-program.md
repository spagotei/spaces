# Spaces Partner Program

The Spaces Partner Program recognizes communities, creators, organizations, and other Spaces that demonstrate sustained quality, safety, activity, and positive contribution to the platform. Partner status is a privilege, not a paid entitlement.

## How to apply
Space owners may apply from the Partner page when applications are open. Applications may request the Space ID, owner account, community purpose, moderation structure, activity information, public links, and examples of the community's work.

## Core eligibility
A Partner candidate should:

- have a clear and genuine community purpose;
- maintain active, responsible moderation;
- comply with the Terms and Community Guidelines;
- have no recent severe safety violations or credible unresolved abuse patterns;
- maintain ownership and accurate account information;
- protect moderator accounts with strong authentication where available; and
- provide original, lawful, and useful community content.

Spaces may apply additional measurable requirements by program phase. Requirements should be published before they are enforced and may differ between Partner tiers.

## Review
Authorized Spaces staff review applications using safety, activity, moderation, authenticity, and community-quality signals. Approval is not automatic. Staff may request additional information.

## Partner benefits
Partner Spaces may receive a Partner identity badge, discovery or featured placement, access to partner communications, program events, early feature opportunities, or other benefits Spaces chooses to offer. Benefits may change and are not guaranteed.

## Maintaining status
Partners must continue meeting program standards. Spaces may issue warnings, request remediation, pause benefits, or remove Partner status when standards are no longer met.

## No impersonation
Only Spaces may grant Partner status. A Space owner, moderator, or third party may not self-assign or represent itself as a Spaces Partner.
