SPACES 0.0.17 — SUPPORT, CHANNELS & PEOPLE
==========================================

SUPPORT CONSOLE
---------------
- Support Console is platform-role gated: Founder, Staff and Support only.
- Rebuilt the small panel into a full operational console.
- Added queue views for:
  - player reports
  - Space reports
  - bug reports
  - official Support DM history
- Existing player report system is now actionable from the console.
- Staff can review/dismiss reports and take account action within platform hierarchy.
- Added platform Ban / Unban controls.
- Added official Support DMs with subject + message and audit history.
- Added Space Restrict / Unrestrict actions. Restricted Spaces become read-only for normal members while still viewable.
- Added permanent Space deletion for authorized Support roles with typed Space-name confirmation.
- Founder/Staff/Support hierarchy is enforced by the Worker, not just hidden in the UI.
- Spaces - Hub cannot be reported, restricted or deleted.

REPORTING
---------
- Any signed-in member can right-click/hold a Space in their rail and choose Report Space.
- Account Settings -> Content & Safety now includes Report a Spaces bug.
- Existing member profile Report action continues to feed Player reports.
- Support cases are persisted in D1 and survive app restarts/deploys.

CHANNELS
--------
- Added Pin channel / Unpin channel to channel context menus.
- Pinned channels appear directly below Overview for fast access.
- Channel groups can now collapse independently per Space:
  - Pinned
  - Information
  - Conversations
  - Notes
  - Staff Channels
  - Space tools
- Collapse state and pinned channels persist in local app preferences.

SPACE SETTINGS
--------------
- Space picture is now the FIRST card in the Appearance/background grid.
- It opens the existing image crop editor and saves through normal Space identity settings.
- Works for regular Spaces and Spaces - Hub when Founder has management access.

PEOPLE
------
- Search now sits directly under "Everyone in this Space, at a glance" instead of leaving empty header space.
- Member bubbles stay in their layout positions.
- Avatars/glows get a subtle independent floating animation so the People page feels alive without cards drifting around.
- Existing click-to-open floating member profile remains.

ACCOUNT PRESENCE
----------------
- Bottom-left account menu now visibly overlays the current presence on the profile picture.
- Current state is also labeled in the account header where there was unused space.
- Online, Idle, Do Not Disturb and Invisible continue using the same saved presence state.

BACKEND / DATABASE
------------------
- New required migration: 0015_support_cases_space_restrictions.sql
- New D1 tables:
  - support_cases
  - support_messages
  - space_restrictions
- Worker API health version bumped to 6.0.
- Added support-case, Support DM and Space restriction health flags.
- Deployment helper intentionally uses `wrangler d1 execute --file` because this account previously hit Cloudflare code 7403 through `d1 migrations apply` while direct execution worked.

RESPONSIVE
----------
- Full Support Console adapts to desktop, tablet and phone.
- Mobile Support Console becomes a full-screen operations sheet.
- People header/search and action layouts are responsive.
